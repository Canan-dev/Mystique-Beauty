## 🚀 How to Open & Test

1. Simply double-click `index.html` or open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
2. To test the mobile view:
   - Press `F12` in Chrome/Edge, click the **Toggle Device Toolbar** icon (or `Ctrl + Shift + M`), and select **iPhone 12/14/15** or **Pixel 7**.
3. To test booking:
   - Fill in your name, pick a date & time, and click **"Send Booking to WhatsApp"**. It will launch WhatsApp with your pre-formatted appointment request to `+256 786 716564`!
